# Mock Question Bank Filter Rules

- Medium has exactly three choices: Common (MAT), English, Assamese.
- Common (MAT) exposes the five MAT parts from MOCK_PLAN. Topic and Passage are hidden.
- English/Assamese expose exactly four Part choices: Arithmetic, EVS-MCQ, ENS-Passage, Language.
- Topic and Passage are available for English/Assamese. Passage options are restricted to the selected language.
- Changing Medium resets Part, Topic and Passage. Changing Part resets Topic and Passage.
- Existing search/status/edit/activate/deactivate/delete functions are preserved.
